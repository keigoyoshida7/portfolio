Editorial reference note

The supplied memo and the source-verification context are recorded separately in this note. EN displays their English translation; JP displays the Japanese record. A cited source supports only the claims identified in the verification context.

Original note Le Corbusier applied the New Testament saying “Put new wine into new wineskins” from Matthew to architectural design, advocating L’Esprit Nouveau (“the new spirit”) for the architecture of a new age. Source check Matthew 9:17 contains the metaphor of putting new wine into fresh wineskins. The original argument of Le Corbusier’s Towards an Architecture (1923) discusses a new spirit and new plans for the house and city suited to modern life. The Esprit Nouveau Pavilion offers an architectural example of this thinking. A primary source confirming the original note’s attribution—that Le Corbusier himself directly used this biblical verse in architectural design—has not yet been identified. The supplied note is retained alongside the sources that could be verified.
