Archived reference note

English translation of the archived note. JP shows the original text. Source links and literal code are retained; the translation does not update or independently verify the source claims.

Tenchusatsu is a period considered in Sanmeigaku and the Four Pillars of Destiny to be “a time when heaven does not favor you,” during which fortune becomes unstable. It comes equally to everyone at intervals such as 2 years every 12 years, 2 months every 12 months, and 2 days every 12 days; annual Tenchusatsu is considered particularly influential. Rather than thinking “bad things will happen,” this period can be seen as a “recharging/hibernation period” for avoiding new starts and reflecting on yourself, offering an opportunity to reconsider how you live.
天中殺とは、算命学や四柱推命において「天が味方しない時期」とされ、運気が不安定になる期間のことです。これは12年に2年、12ヶ月に2ヶ月、12日に2日間といったペースで誰にでも平等に訪れ、特に年の天中殺は影響が大きいとされます。この時期は「悪いことが起こる」と考えるよりも、新しいスタートを避け、自分を見つめ直す「充電・休眠期」と捉え、生き方を見直すチャンスとすることもできます。﻿
