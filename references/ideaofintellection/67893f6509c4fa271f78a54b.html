Archived reference note

English translation of the archived note. JP shows the original text. Source links and literal code are retained; the translation does not update or independently verify the source claims.

Meaning: Sublimity felt when encountering something overwhelming and beyond comprehension, a mixture of fear and attraction. Example: Awe felt when seeing high mountains or a rough sea.
意味: 圧倒的で理解を超えたものに触れたときに感じる崇高さ、恐怖と魅力の混ざった感覚。 例: 高山や荒々しい海を見たときの畏敬の念。
