Archived reference note

English translation of the archived note. JP shows the original text. Source links and literal code are retained; the translation does not update or independently verify the source claims.

tone language Languages recognized as having established tone patterns as a social convention that form a distinctive system—that is, tonemes. Chinese, Vietnamese, Thai, Serbo-Croatian, Africa’s Efik, Igbo, and Yoruba, and Mexico’s Mixtec and Mazatec. #sound
tone language 社会習慣として一定した声調の型をもち，それらが弁別的体系をなす，すなわち声調素を有すると認定される言語をいう。 中国語，ベトナム語，タイ語，セルボ＝クロアチア語，アフリカのエフィク語，イボ語，ヨルバ語，メキシコのミシュテカ語，マサテカ語 #sound
