Archived reference note

English translation of the archived note. JP shows the original text. Source links and literal code are retained; the translation does not update or independently verify the source claims.

Artworks with feedback mechanisms moving in response to environmental stimuli such as sound, voices, light, wind, and temperature changes. A type of kinetic art, differing in that movement does not come from the work's own apparatus.
音や声，光，風，温度の変化など外部環境の刺激によって動く，フィードバック機構を備えた芸術作品。 キネティック・アートの一種だが，作品自体の装置によって動くのでないところが異なる。
