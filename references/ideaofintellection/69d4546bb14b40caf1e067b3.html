Archived reference note

English translation of the archived note. JP shows the original text. Source links and literal code are retained; the translation does not update or independently verify the source claims.

Ergon (Greek: ἔργον) is an ancient Greek noun meaning “work,” “labor,” “action,” or “artwork.” Beyond physical labor, it also encompasses results, functions, and roles, and is an extremely important root giving rise to modern “energy” and “ergonomics.”
Ergon（ギリシャ語：ἔργον）は、古代ギリシャ語で「仕事」「労働」「行為」「作品」を意味する名詞です。単なる物理的な労働だけでなく、成果や機能、役割という意味合いも含み、現代のエネルギー（Energy）やエルゴノミクス（人間工学）の語源となった非常に重要な語幹です。
