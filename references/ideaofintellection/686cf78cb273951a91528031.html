English translation of the archived note. JP shows the original text. Source links and literal code are retained; the translation does not update or independently verify the source claims.

The shamisen's “sawari” is a distinctive buzzing sound generated when a string touches the neck under specific conditions. An important element characterizing its timbre, it is adjusted to produce the sound intentionally.
三味線の「さわり」とは、特定の条件下で糸が棹に触れることで発生する独特のビビリ音のことです。これは、三味線の音色を特徴づける重要な要素の一つであり、意図的にその音を出すように調整されます。﻿
