https://scrapbox.io/files/66b3b42804e094001c35be73.png The Aspen Movie Map was a hypermedia system developed at MIT allowing users to take virtual tours of Aspen, Colorado. It was developed in 1978 with ARPA funding by a team working with Andrew Lippman. #media
