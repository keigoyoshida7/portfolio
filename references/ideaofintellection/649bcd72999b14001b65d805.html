English translation of the archived note. JP shows the original text. Source links and literal code are retained; the translation does not update or independently verify the source claims.

Obligate means “necessarily” and is used mainly in biology in phrases such as: obligate aerobes, organisms unable to survive without oxygen; obligate anaerobes, organisms unable to survive in the presence of oxygen.
obligate は「必然的に」を意味し、主に生物学で次のようなフレーズで使用されます。 酸素なしでは生存できない生物である偏性好気性生物 酸素の存在下では生存できない生物である偏性嫌気性菌
