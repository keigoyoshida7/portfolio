Archived reference note

English translation of the archived note. JP shows the original text. Source links and literal code are retained; the translation does not update or independently verify the source claims.

Loss of interest in things that have become too familiar also occurs in human relationships. Someone admired from afar can become uninteresting upon closer acquaintance. This is expressed by the proverb “No man is a hero to his valet.” #word
身近になりすぎたものへの関心が失われるのは、人間関係でも見られる現象です。 遠くから眺めていた人が、親しくなってみるとおもしろくなくなることがあります。 これは、「従僕に英雄なし」ということわざに表されています。 #word
