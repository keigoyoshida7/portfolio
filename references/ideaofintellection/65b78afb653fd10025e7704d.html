Archived reference note

English translation of the archived note. JP shows the original text. Source links and literal code are retained; the translation does not update or independently verify the source claims.

Because its color is translucent rather than pure white like shell white, it is used not as white paint but mixed with other pigments or applied thinly to a substrate like an effect pigment to create sparkle. Its other effects are diverse, and it is said to have been used for electrical insulation, heat-resistant materials, and, in ancient times, Chinese medicines and insect repellents.
胡粉のように真っ白ではなく半透明な色のため、白の絵具としてではなく他の顔料と混ぜたり、エフェクト顔料のように基底材に薄く塗って煌めきを出す用途で使われています。 それ以外にも効果は多岐に及び、電気絶縁や耐熱材料、古代では漢方薬や防虫材としても使われていたそうです。
