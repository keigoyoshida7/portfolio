Vail, A/V performance at
Future Frequencies Festival 2026

"Vail" is an audiovisual performance that explores the presence of shadows, rain, and anonymous forms through sound and visuals.
It was performed at Future Frequencies Festival 2026 on July 11, 2026.

The sound performance incorporated Neutone morpho, neu.grain, and neu.rhythm by Neutone Inc.
