Muscle Synergy of A Lower Limb in Drummers'Dystonia: A Case Study. at ICMPC and APSCOM

A science research poster, Muscle Synergy of A Lower Limb in Drummers'Dystonia: A Case Study was presented at The Joint Conference of the 17th International Conference on Music Perception and Cognition and the 7th Conference of the Asia-Pacific Society for the Cognitive Sciences of Music (ICMPC17-APSCOM7).

Sata S., Honda K., Yamaguchi S., Komine M., Yoshida K., Kim S., Kashino M., Hagio S., & Fujii S
