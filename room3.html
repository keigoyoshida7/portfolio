Flying Tokyo 2024

Flying Tokyo 2024 is an audiovisual and media art project by Keigo Yoshida, developed with support from Japan’s Ministry of Economy, Trade and Industry (METI) and Rhizomatiks.
The project began on June 17, 2024.
