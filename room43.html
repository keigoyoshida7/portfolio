Audio Buffer Tokyo at FIL

Audio Buffer Tokyo is an open event for experimental audio practices, technologies, and works in progress, co-organized by Keigo Yoshida and Daito Manabe.
As the Tokyo edition of Audio Buffer London, it brings together artists, musicians, researchers, developers, and others working with audio to test, share, and discuss projects, prototypes, software, systems, and ideas.
Rather than focusing only on finished works, the event provides a space to exchange ideas and explore new possibilities in audio together.

FIL's audio facilities were used alongside a multichannel setup consisting of 12 Musikelectronic Geithain monitor speakers suspended from the ceiling.
